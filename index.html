    Baked with Purpose

    Every batch creates job training and employment opportunities for people with intellectual and developmental differences.

    Campus Cookies began with a group of Career Campus students with intellectual and developmental differences who turned a college entrepreneurship class into a real-world business. Today, each batch creates opportunities to build culinary and baking skills through hands-on study, internships, and paid employment.
