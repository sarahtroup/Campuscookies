Baked with Purpose

Our gourmet cookies are baked with purpose, creating job training and employment opportunities for people with intellectual and developmental differences.

Campus Cookies began with a group of Career Campus students with intellectual and developmental differences who turned a college entrepreneurship class into a real-world business idea. Today, each batch creates opportunities to build culinary and baking skills through hands-on study, internships, and paid employment.
